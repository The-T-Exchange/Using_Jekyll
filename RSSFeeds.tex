\chapter{RSS Feeds}\label{chap: RSS Feeds}

If you look at the website---the live on or the demo we have been playing with---you will see, near the bottom of the home page, the words ``Subscribe via RSS''. If you click the ``via RSS'' link, you will see a page showing some XML versions of the website.

If you have an RSS reader, you can copy the link's location and paste it into your RSS reader. You will now see any changes made to the website without having to login and look for them---they come to you!

RSS Readers are handy if you follow a number of websites and rather than logging into each one individually to see what's new or updated, you simply subscribe to them in your RSS Reader and all the changes come to you in one place.

\emph{RSS Guard} and \emph{Thunderbird Mail} are two examples of cross-platform RSS Readers. With Thunderbird, you get your emails and RSS Feed all in one place.
